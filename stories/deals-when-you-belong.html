The deals you only get when you belong

Why relationships and Spanish are the real currency here.

Lucas
September 2026 · 2 min read

A few months ago, my neighbor came by with a question: did I want to buy the piece of land next to mine? Not a listing, not an agent, not a price on a website. Just a neighbor, offering first to the person next door. That's how the best deals happen here, and it's exactly the kind of deal a foreign buyer will never hear about on their own.

The deal

My main property is about 1.3 hectares. The piece he offered connects right to it: a little over two acres. The price? 15,000 soles, somewhere around 4,000 US dollars.

Two-plus acres for four thousand dollars, directly adjoining land I already owned. And here's the kicker: there are now water and electricity hookups about 30 meters away from it. On paper, that should push the price up. I still got it for a song.

Why a stranger never gets this price

If my neighbor had sold that plot to an outsider, someone he didn't know or someone who didn't speak Spanish well, it would almost certainly have cost far more. Maybe it would never have been offered at all.

That's not because anyone's trying to cheat you. It's how a relationship-based market works. Sellers offer first to the people they trust, at prices that reflect that trust. Language matters too: the whole conversation happened in Spanish, neighbor to neighbor. A buyer flying in for a weekend, working through a translator, simply isn't in that circle yet.

The real currency here is relationships and language.

The real currency here

Everyone focuses on price per square meter. But in a place like this, the real currency is relationships and language. The deals that never hit a website, like the neighbor's plot or the family selling to someone they trust, are the best ones, and they only flow to people who belong to the community.

That's the whole reason I do this. When you work with someone who lives here, speaks the language, and is already the trusted neighbor, you don't just get help with paperwork. You get access to a market that stays invisible to everyone else.
