Services & pricing

Three ways to work together

My fee matches the work involved. Someone buying a small plot I already know shouldn't pay the same as someone who needs weeks of searching.

Travel expenses are reimbursed at cost. Every fee is agreed in writing before any work begins. I'm not a lawyer; a licensed Peruvian real estate attorney handles the legal side of every purchase. How the fees work

A slice of the rainforest is a flat fee. The land is already known to me, so the work is predictable.

The search and Start to finish have a base fee up front once you're serious, plus a smaller success fee at closing. The base fee covers the scouting itself, which has real value even when it saves you from a bad purchase.

If a deal falls through because the title has a problem or the land isn't right, we talk through your options before any more money moves.
