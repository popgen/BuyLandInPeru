PHOTO SLOTS — Buy Land In Peru
================================
Drop your photos into this "images" folder using EXACTLY these file names
(lowercase, .jpg). Each one appears automatically on the pages listed.
Until a photo is added, the site shows a green placeholder with a label.

Tip: resize to about 1600-2000 px wide and save as JPG (under ~400 KB) so pages load fast.
