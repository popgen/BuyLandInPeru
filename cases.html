Purchases, before and after citizenship

I have bought as a foreigner and again as a Peruvian citizen, and I have sold using local price knowledge. I am not going to invent the narrative of those deals. The blanks below are for my own words, added when I am ready to publish them. Addresses, prices, and profits stay off this page unless I deliberately put them in.

What these are not

They are not client case studies. I do not have permissioned testimonials on the site yet. Those placeholders live on the about page. They are not a track record you can audit, because I have not published the documents. Treat the essays in the journal as the teaching, and these boxes as mine to fill.
