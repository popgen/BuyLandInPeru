The person, and the limits

About this work

Buy Land in Peru is this guide: I help people through a purchase in Peru, and I am on the ground when they cannot.

What is true

I was born in the United States. I later became a naturalized Peruvian citizen. I bought property before that, and I bought property after. I have lived in various parts of the country. I have bought and sold for a profit because I knew local price ranges. I am not publishing the prices, the profits, or the addresses. There is a page waiting for those stories in my own words, and it is labeled as waiting.

I am not your lawyer, your notary, or your tax advisor. I do not present myself as a registered agente inmobiliario. The legal transaction is done with an independent abogado and an independent notario. [Placeholder: independent abogado and notario in the region. Names go here when I choose to publish them.]

What I do

  • Help you decide what kind of life you are shopping for, including a rental first.
  • Match climate, hospitals, and budget to places I can describe honestly.
  • Search, including off-market, and compare a price with local ranges.
  • Scout: photos, video, neighbors, boundaries, and the hours listings skip.
  • Sit with you through the sequence and introduce the independent professionals.
  • Afterward, caretaker check-ins and a clear eye on construction. Not a management company.

What I do not do

  • Give legal, tax, immigration, or investment advice.
  • Guarantee a title, a profit, a rental yield, or a visa. Buying property does not grant residency. Land values can fall.
  • Claim I can walk every region of Peru. Other regions happen through a trusted local contact when that is the honest fit.
  • Publish a commission policy. Fees are quoted for a stated service after intake.
  • Remove squatters, pressure a neighbor, or “fix” a file that your lawyer says is bad.
