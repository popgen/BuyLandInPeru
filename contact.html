Contact

Tell me about the piece of Peru you've been dreaming about.

The first call is free, with no pressure and no obligation. Send me a message below, or email me directly at info [at] buylandinperu.com.

Send a message

Send me a message

I'll reply here, and you'll get a quick confirmation that your message arrived.

Your details are only used to reply to you. See the privacy note.

What helps me help you

No need for anything polished. A few lines help me give you a useful answer the first time:

  • What you picture: a small plot, a homestead, a house, farmland, or land to simply hold
  • Where, if you know (or "somewhere in the jungle" is fine)
  • A rough budget in dollars
  • Your timeline
  • Whether you'd like to visit or buy remotely

What happens next

  1. I read it myself

    There's no call center and no sales team. Every message comes to me.

  2. We set up a free call

    We talk through what you want and I tell you honestly what's realistic.

  3. You decide

    If it's a fit, we agree on a tier and fee in writing. If not, you've lost nothing.

Founding clients: fee waived

I'm taking my first three clients at cost. You cover expenses only, I waive my fee, and in return I ask for an honest review of how it went.
